What the data shows

4.2 hrs → 2.4 days

NYPD makes the city look fast

NYPD handles 47% of requests, mostly parking and noise, and closes half of them within 1.4 hours. Without NYPD, the median wait jumps from 4.2 hours to 2.4 days, and the share closed within a week drops from 78% to 57%.

17.5% of volume, 35% of backlog

Housing is where the backlog lives

HPD receives about one in six requests but holds 17,144 of the 48,553 still open. Fewer than half of HPD requests close within a week, and unsanitary conditions, plumbing, and paint and plaster are its biggest open categories.

3,241 requests waiting 21+ days

Parks has the oldest queue

DPR has more requests older than three weeks than any other agency. Only 10% of overgrown tree and branch reports closed within seven days.

55.8% to 58.2% without NYPD

Borough gaps are mostly mix

Queens looks fastest overall, but more than half its requests go to NYPD. Comparing only non-police requests, four boroughs land within 2.4 points of each other. Staten Island leads at 63%.

When requests come in

Weekday requests peak late morning. Weekend volume peaks around 10 p.m., when noise complaints make up 58% of requests, versus 22% across the month.

What I would recommend

Report speed by agency, not as one citywide number

A single median mixes 90-minute parking calls with week-long housing repairs. Leaders should see each agency against its own target.

Work the HPD and Parks queues oldest first

About 5,900 HPD and Parks requests had waited three weeks or more. An aging review would show which are stuck and which only need a status update.

Check how helicopter noise reports are routed

All 484 reports sent to EDC were still open. Either they close outside 311 or nobody owns them, and both are worth fixing.

Match staffing to when requests arrive

Intake peaks late morning on weekdays and late at night on weekends. Staffing and dispatch schedules should follow that curve.

Method and data notes

Source
NYC Open Data, 311 Service Requests (dataset erm2-nwe9). All requests opened Sep 1–30, 2026, with status as of the latest closure in the file (Oct 3, 2026, 1:49 a.m.).
Resolved
A request counts as resolved when its status is Closed and its closed time is after its open time. "Closed" means the agency closed the ticket, which does not always mean the problem was fixed.
Excluded from timing
requests closed in the same second they opened and "closed" before they opened. They stay in volume counts but not in time-to-close metrics ( of requests).
7-day closure rate
Only requests opened by Sep 25 count, so each one had at least seven full days to close. Late-month requests would otherwise make every agency look slower.
Medians
Median and 90th-percentile times use the nearest-rank method, written in SQL with window functions.
Tools
SQL (SQLite) for every metric, Python (pandas) to run the queries and build this page.
